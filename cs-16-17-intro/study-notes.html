Ohio Constitution and Citizenship — Introduction Study Notes

CS 16 · 1

Popular sovereignty means the people are the source of legitimate governmental power.

CS 16 · 3

Checks and balances give branches ways to limit or respond to other branches.

CS 16 · 4

Limited government subjects officials and institutions to constitutional limits.

CS 16 · 6

Elections allow the people to exercise political authority through representatives.

CS 16 · 13

Petition signatures help qualify a measure for ballot consideration; they do not decide the vote.

CS 16 · 15

Ohio imposes term limits on state legislators; the U.S. Constitution does not impose them on Congress.

CS 16 · 17

Both systems divide governmental responsibilities among three branches.

CS 16 · 20

State constitutions establish state institutions and protections subject to the U.S. Constitution.

CS 16 · 26

Constitutions provide the governing framework and limit ordinary legislation.

CS 16 · 28

Limited government means officials are bound by the law and constitution.

CS 17 · 29

Voting helps citizens select leaders and decide issues submitted to voters.

CS 17 · 30

Constituents can communicate with lawmakers during the legislative process.

CS 17 · 31

Local meetings offer opportunities to bring concerns and solutions to officials.

CS 17 · 32

School board meetings allow residents to follow local education governance and participate appropriately.

CS 17 · 33

Community volunteer work can support local public services and goals.

CS 17 · 34

Neighborhood watch programs can help residents cooperate with local public safety efforts.

CS 17 · 35

Civic participation includes hearings, committees, and other lawful public activities.

CS 17 · 36

Informed participation begins with understanding the policy and available evidence.

CS 17 · 38

Eligible residents can serve in the Ohio National Guard as a form of public service.

CS 17 · 39

Ohio election rules include petition and nomination processes for ballot access.

CS 17 · 40

Attending and offering practical input helps local officials consider residents’ needs.
